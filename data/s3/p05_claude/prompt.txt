Imagine a school cafeteria gives you digital money for food, but to encourage you to spend it quickly, they use a "decay" rule. 
For the first 7 days, your money is completely safe, but starting on day 8, you lose exactly 10% of whatever money you have left 
every single day. Create a Python function that calculates how much money a student has left. The code must take the starting 
amount of money and the number of days that have passed as inputs, use this decay rule to calculate the remaining balance, 
and return the final answer as a decimal number rounded to exactly two places. For this function, assume the student has not 
spent any of the money; you are only calculating how the original amount shrinks due to time passing.
