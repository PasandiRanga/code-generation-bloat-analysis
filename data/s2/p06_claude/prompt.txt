There is a space delivery company that uses following text format for records. 
SHIPID:WEIGHT:DESTINATION. Following are the rules to consider a record being 
valid or not. 
1. All three parts must be separated with colons 
2. SHIPID must be six uppercase letters 
3. WEIGHT must be a positive whole number 
4. DESTINATION should be a single word without spaces 
Write a python function to take a record as input, validate against above rules 
and outputs true or false after validation.
