imagine a fictional game called Zorblax. In this game, players score points by 
collecting "zaps" and "blitzes." Every zap is worth 7 points, and every blitz 
is worth 13 points. However, there is a special combo rule: if a player collects 
strictly more blitzes than zaps in a single round, the points they get from all 
of their zaps are doubled. Give me a Python function that takes the number of zaps 
and the number of blitzes as inputs, calculates the final score based on these 
rules, and returns the total points.
